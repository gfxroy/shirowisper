ShiroWisper 1.0.0
Mac dictation. Hold a key, speak, and the words paste into the app you were using.

macOS 13 or later. Apple silicon.

After you download
macOS blocks an app it has not seen before. This takes a minute the first time.

1. Move ShiroWisper into Applications.
2. Right-click ShiroWisper, choose Open, then Open again. If macOS still blocks it, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.
3. Open ShiroWisper and allow the microphone.
4. In System Settings, Privacy & Security, Accessibility, turn ShiroWisper on. If a row named Python is listed, leave it off.
5. Quit ShiroWisper and open it again. The hotkey starts listening after that.
6. In Settings, paste a Gemini key from https://aistudio.google.com/apikey. Choose Test key, then Save settings.

If it hears you and nothing pastes, turn Accessibility off and on, then quit and reopen.

Speech is sent to Google with that key. The key stays on your Mac, in a file only your account can read.

Hold Fn to dictate. Double-tap Fn for hands-free, then tap once to stop. Select text and hold F9 to edit it by voice. Say "scratch that" to drop the last paste. The keys can be changed in Settings.

Windows and Linux are not available.
